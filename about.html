Meet the Photographer

Hello! I am Kayla, a visual storyteller based in Downtown Ottawa. My goal is to capture real emotion, timeless landscapes, and authentic moments.

My journey began over a decade ago with oldschool Nikon cameras, evolving into modern digital storytelling across portraiture and commercial assignments.

Creative Philosophy

Authenticity First

I prioritize natural poses and unscripted interactions over overly staged compositions.

Mastery of Light

Whether working in a studio or on location, mastering ambient and directional light forms the core of my craft.
